Research agenda

Research

Understanding and simulating networked societies, from the structure of social interactions to LLM agents inhabiting digital twins of online platforms.

01

Computational Social Science

Online debates, polarization and echo chambers, polluted information environments and online well-being, studied through large-scale social data.

02

Network Science

Dynamic, higher-order and feature-rich network models; community discovery, diffusion and opinion dynamics on and of complex networks.

03

Generative AI

How large language models reason, agree and persuade: cognitive biases, opinion dynamics among LLM agents and the interplay between human and machine cognition.

04

Social Twins

LLM-powered digital twins of social media platforms to run controlled, reproducible experiments on recommender systems, moderation and information spread.
