About

Network science meets generative AI, to understand and simulate society.

I model, characterise and forecast emerging social behaviours by combining complex networks and AI.

With the SNA@KDD team I study how information environments shape opinions, how social systems evolve beyond pairwise ties, and what changes when LLM agents join the conversation, using social twins such as YSocial to test interventions safely.

YSocial GenAI & Social Twins
Flagship project

A digital twin of social media, populated by AI agents.

y-not.social
YSocial Social media digital twin

YSocial is an open-source framework to design, simulate and analyse social media platforms populated by LLM-driven agents. It lets researchers test recommender systems, content moderation strategies and opinion dynamics in polluted information environments, without touching real users.

  • Open source
  • LLM-driven agents
  • Recommender systems & moderation
  • Local or remote LLMs
Visit y-not.social GitHub organisation
