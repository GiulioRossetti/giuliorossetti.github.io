I am a **Senior Researcher** at the Institute of Information Science and Technologies "A. Faedo" of the Italian National Research Council ([CNR-ISTI](https://www.isti.cnr.it/)) and **External Professor** of Network Science at the [University of Pisa](https://di.unipi.it/). Within the [KDD Lab](http://kdd.isti.cnr.it/), a joint research team of the University of Pisa, CNR-ISTI and Scuola Normale Superiore, I coordinate the research on **Complex Network Analysis** (the SNA@KDD team). I am a member of the Doctoral Collegium of the Italian National PhD in Artificial Intelligence for Society.

My research models, characterises and forecasts emerging social behaviours by combining complex networks with AI/ML. Today this means three intertwined questions:

- **How do information environments shape opinions?** Polarization, echo chambers and polluted information ecosystems, but also supportive online communities.
- **How do networks evolve, beyond pairwise ties?** Dynamic, higher-order and feature-rich models of social systems.
- **What happens when LLMs join the conversation?** Their biases and cognition, how they persuade and agree, and how *social twins* of online platforms let us test interventions safely.
