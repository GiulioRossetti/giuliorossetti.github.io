I model, characterise and forecast emerging social behaviours by combining **complex networks** and **AI**.

With the SNA@KDD team I study how information environments shape opinions, how social systems evolve beyond pairwise ties, and what changes when **LLM agents** join the conversation, using *social twins* such as [YSocial](https://y-not.social/) to test interventions safely.
