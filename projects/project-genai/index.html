Research line

GenAI & Social Twins

LLM cognition and biases, opinion dynamics among LLM agents, and LLM-powered digital twins of social media platforms such as YSocial.

OverviewTwo complementary directionsYSocialObjectivesPublications

Large Language Models are becoming participants in the social systems we study: they write, recommend, moderate and persuade, and they increasingly interact with each other.

This research line looks at generative AI through the lens of network and computational social science. We study LLMs as social and cognitive actors, and we use them to build social twins, simulated platforms where controlled experiments on online behaviour become possible.

Two complementary directions

LLMs as social and cognitive actors

How LLMs represent knowledge and reproduce or amplify human biases, studied with free-association norms and forma mentis networks; how LLM agents agree, polarize or drift in debates, and when they persuade humans.

Social twins

LLM-powered digital twins of online platforms, where synthetic users with personas, interests and memory post, comment and follow, enabling reproducible and ethically safe experiments on moderation and recommendation.

Our social twin: YSocial

The open-source platform where we put these ideas into practice.

YSocial Social media digital twin

YSocial is an open-source framework to design, simulate and analyse social media platforms populated by LLM-driven agents. It lets researchers test recommender systems, content moderation strategies and opinion dynamics in polluted information environments, without touching real users.

  • Open source
  • LLM-driven agents
  • Recommender systems & moderation
  • Local or remote LLMs

Objectives

  1. 1

    Characterise the cognitive and social biases of LLMs and compare them with human behaviour.

  2. 2

    Model opinion dynamics in populations of LLM agents and in hybrid human–AI networks.

  3. 3

    Build open, reproducible social twins to evaluate the societal impact of platform design choices.
